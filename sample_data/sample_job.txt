Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer
Company: TechCorp AI Solutions
Location: Bangalore / Remote

About the Role:
We are looking for a passionate ML Engineer to join our AI team. You will build and deploy
machine learning models that power our core product features.

Requirements:
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Experience with TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn
- Knowledge of NLP techniques and transformer models
- Familiarity with LLMs, prompt engineering, and OpenAI or Groq APIs
- Experience with data processing using Pandas and NumPy
- Familiarity with Git, CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, Docker)
- Knowledge of REST APIs and model deployment with Flask or FastAPI or Streamlit
- Understanding of feature engineering, model evaluation, and hyperparameter tuning
- Bonus: Experience with Hugging Face Transformers, RAG pipelines, or LangChain
- Bonus: Cloud experience (AWS, GCP, Azure)

Responsibilities:
- Build and deploy end-to-end ML pipelines
- Develop NLP and computer vision models
- Work with LLMs and generative AI tools
- Collaborate with data engineers and software teams
- Monitor model performance in production

Preferred Qualifications:
- B.Tech or M.Tech in Computer Science, AI, Data Science, or related field
- 1-2 years of internship or work experience
- Published research or open-source contributions are a plus
